What makes it work

  • Net pay firstThe figure people look for leads the page, in words too, with the bank it went to and a bar showing where the gross went.
  • Every line explainedPension and NHF show their base (8% of ₦255,000; 2.5% of basic). Loans show the instalment and balance left.
  • Tax shown, not just chargedTaxable pay, reliefs and bands to date, then tax due less tax already paid. It reconciles to the kobo, even in a bonus month, so HR stops fielding "why is my tax this?" questions.
  • Year to dateThe totals employees need for tax records, loan and visa applications.
  • Employer contributionsShown for transparency and clearly marked as not deducted. Can be switched off per company.
  • Plain-English insightsWorked out once when the payslip is issued and stored with it. Nothing predictive, no advice.
  • VerifiableA QR code and code let a bank or landlord confirm the payslip is genuine. A fingerprint of the figures means an edited copy fails.
  • One A4 pagePrints and saves as PDF in greyscale-safe colours, in the company's brand colour.
